# League Rules
League Rules is the rules that the tsm players should follow so they can play their matches without violating the rules and also knowing how tsm works

## 1. Pitch:
- 1.0.0: The Match Must be **4v4, 4v3, 3v3** and not **4v2, 3v2, 2v2**
  - note: if the match started as 4v2 then its fft win to the team with 4 players, if 3v2 the same thing fft win for the team with 3 players, but if it was 2v2 then fft draw for the both teams
- 1.0.1: The Match Must start with a referee, without the referee the match won't start and won't played and the manager or the player of the team that gonna play the match is not allowed to ref his own game
  - note: the manager or the player can ref his own game only if the opponent team accepted and there is no active referees
- 1.0.2: The Private Server Hoster has to join the private server first and then sends the link of the private server in the matchcall allowing the players who is in lineups of both teams to enter
  - (note: if the hoster was north american and the host should be european or anything else then the private server owner has to send the private server to the player who is european via dms and not in the matchcall)
- 1.0.3: Penalty Shootouts and Sudden Death does NOT count even if the match was a cup
- 1.0.4: To Decide the Hoster, Both Teams has to choose between **Heads or Tails** and the winner is gonna choose the region host in which leg (the coinflip winner can not do double eu by himself without the opponent approval)
- 1.0.5: The Number of players on the private server should be **__7/10, 8/10, 9/10, 10/10__** and not **__1/10, 2/10, 3/10, 4/10, 5/10, 6/10__** and the players that should be in the private server are:
  - Referee (REQUIRED 1/1)
  - Streamer (OPTIONAL 1/1)
  - Team 1 (REQUIRED ?/4)
  - Team 2 (REQUIRED ?/4)
- 1.0.6: the team has to play with 4 players in the match, but if the number of players became 2 players for 5 mins then its fft win to the opponent team
- 1.0.7: the team has to send a final lineup, when sending a lineup you can't change it only if one of your players is leaving but changing player while no players in lineups wants to leave is not allowed

## 2. Fair-Play
- 2.0.0: Cheating (using executors like delta/solara and more) is **__FORBIDDEN__** and if you got caught using a executor even if you downloaded it for another game rather than tfb, you will still get banned
  - (note: read [Cheating](sanctions))
- 2.0.1: There is a allowed fastflag programs and the **__FORBIDDEN__** ones, the pc ones are: **Bloxstrap and Fishstrap**, the android and ios ones are: **NONE (note: using any android/ios fastflag program could result in a heavy punishment)**
- 2.0.2: Teaming with the opponent team to disqualify a team from a competition could result a **__DISQUALIFY__** to both teams and heavy punishment for who participated in the teaming
- 2.0.3: if you bribed a referee to win a match or doing something bad to the opponent team will get you banned both of you and the referee and fft win to the opponent team

## 3. Contracts
- 3.0.0: The Contract Formats must be like the ones at [Formats]()
- 3.0.1: The Manager or Assistant Manager must show a proof of signing the player and sending it to **contract-request** channel
  - (note: termination of contract and auto-release does not need a proof, njc activiation needs a proof only if the opponent manager njc activiated the player that in another team)
- 3.0.2: There is 7 Day Rule and 24 Hours Rule, 24 Hours Rule lets the player releases himself automatically from the team even if he was nrc but if the 24 hours passed then he needs to ask the manager for a release, 7 Day Rule is different there it means that the player has to stay in the team that he wants to leave, but if he was nrc he will stay stuck forever only if the manager released him, but if the player is signed as NJC then he can leave after the 7 Days ends
- 3.0.3: Emergency Signs and Loans are not allowed anymore (due to **Argentina vs Portugal** and **Peru vs Canada** dramas)
- 3.0.4: You can not sign players 10mins before and after the match
