# League Rules
League Rules is the rules that the tsm players should follow so they can play their matches without violating the rules and also knowing how tsm works

## 1. Main Rules
- 1.0.0: The Match Must be **4v4, 4v3, 3v3** and not **4v2, 3v2, 2v2**
  - note: if the match started as 4v2 then its fft win to the team with 4 players, if 3v2 the same thing fft win for the team with 3 players, but if it was 2v2 then fft draw for the both teams
- 1.0.1: if a team failed to show up in the matchcall 15minutes after the match starts, the fft win will be awarded to the opponent team
- 1.0.2: if both teams failed to show up in the matchcall 15minutes after the match starts, fft draw for both teams
- 1.0.3: The Match Must start with a referee, without the referee the match won't start and won't played and the manager or the player of the team that gonna play the match is not allowed to ref his own game
  - note: if there was no referee, both teams has to reschudele their match
- 1.0.4: The Private Server Hoster has to join the private server first and then sends the link of the private server in the matchcall allowing the players who is in lineups of both teams to enter
  - (note: if the hoster was north american and the host should be european or anything else then the private server owner has to send the private server to the player who is european via dms and not in the matchcall)
- 1.0.5: Penalty Shootouts and Sudden Death does NOT count even if the match was a cup
- 1.0.6: To Decide the Hoster, Both Teams has to choose between **Heads or Tails** and the winner is gonna choose the region host
- 1.0.7: the coinflip winner is allowed only to host one leg (game)
- 1.0.8: if the coinflip winner (host team) couldn't provide or get a private server, the opponent team has to provide a private server and the host team will host the leg (game)
- 1.0.9: The Number of players on the private server should be **__7/10, 8/10, 9/10, 10/10__** and not **__1/10, 2/10, 3/10, 4/10, 5/10, 6/10__** and the players that should be in the private server are:
  - Referee (REQUIRED 1/1)
  - Streamer (OPTIONAL 1/1)
  - Team 1 (REQUIRED ?/4)
  - Team 2 (REQUIRED ?/4)
- 1.1.0: the team has to play with 4 players in the match, but if the number of players became 2 players for 5 mins then its fft win to the opponent team
- 1.1.1: the team has to send a final lineup, when sending a lineup you can't change it only if one of your players is leaving but changing player while no players in lineups wants to leave is not allowed
- 1.1.2: during a game if a team players fall bellow 3 players the game will still continue
- 1.1.3: a substitute player can only play if a goal got scored or before 2nd leg (game) starts
- 1.1.4: if a tie happened in cup-match (knockout stages) 1 more leg will be played until one team wins
- 1.1.5: players in lineups of both teams are the only allowed ones to join the private server
  - (note: any ineligable player entered the match will result in heavy punishments to the player and the manager if he allows him)
- 1.1.6: if your contract wasn't posted at contracts but posted at contract-request then you are allowed to play 
  - (note: if your contract-request was posted 10min after the match starts or before then you arent allowed to play)
- 1.1.7: the cup-tied player is a player who lost between round of 16 and semi finals of a cup game, by giving that role, you aren't allowed to be signed at any team that is still playing the cup after your elimination
- 1.1.8: every region host is allowed

- 3.0.0: The Contract Formats must be like the ones at [Formats]()

  - (note: termination of contract and auto-release does not need a proof, njc activiation needs a proof only if the opponent manager njc activiated the player that in another team)
- 3.0.2: There is 7 Day Rule and 24 Hours Rule, 24 Hours Rule lets the player releases himself automatically from the team even if he was nrc but if the 24 hours passed then he needs to ask the manager for a release, 7 Day Rule is different there it means that the player has to stay in the team that he wants to leave, but if he was nrc he will stay stuck forever only if the manager released him, but if the player is signed as NJC then he can leave after the 7 Days ends
- 3.0.3: Emergency Signs and Loans are not allowed anymore (due to **Argentina vs Portugal** and **Peru vs Canada** dramas)
